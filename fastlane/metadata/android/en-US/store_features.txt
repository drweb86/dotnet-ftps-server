Secure FTP server (FTPS) for sharing files on your Wi-Fi or local network
Photos, videos, and documents stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded to the cloud
Each person gets their own login and a folder they cannot leave
Read-only accounts, or full access to upload, rename, and delete
Logins and files are encrypted in transit with FTP over TLS
Connect from FileZilla, WinSCP, or any client that supports explicit FTPS
Start in minutes: add a user, choose a folder, then connect
A TLS certificate is created for you, or use one you already have
File names in your language are kept, not only English letters
Open source and free to use, with no ads
