Sava FTPS (FTP n'elu TLS) maka ikesa faịlụ n'ụzọ nchekwa n'etiti ngwaọrụ na netwọkụ.

Njirimara:
- **Ikike onye ọrụ** - Njikwa zuru ezu nke ọrụ ịgụ/ide
- **Nchekwa mgbọrọgwụ maka onye ọrụ ọ bụla** - Ndekọ kewapụrụ maka onye ọrụ ọ bụla
- **Nchekwa ụzọ** - Nchebe megide mwakpo directory traversal
- **UTF-8** - Na-akwado mkpụrụedemede e mere ka ọ bụrụ nke mpaghara
- **Asambodo nke onwe** - Mepụta asambodo TLS na-akpaghị aka ma ọ bụ jiri nke gị

Nke a bụ ngwa Android (Kotlin). Enweghị mgbasa ozi. Open source (CC0). Onye ọrụ ọ bụla na-ahọrọ nchekwa site na nhọrọ sistemụ. Ọkwa na-eme ka nnyefe gaa n’ihu mgbe ihuenyo gbanyụrụ.

Ọ chọrọ Android 6.0 ma ọ bụ ọhụrụ.
https://github.com/drweb86/dotnet-ftps-server
