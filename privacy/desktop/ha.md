# Manufar sirri

An sabunta ƙarshe: 13 Satumba 2026


**FTPS Server** by Siarhei Kuchuk

Sunan aikace-aikace: FTPS Server
Sunan mai haɓakawa: Siarhei Kuchuk

Software ɗin sabar FTPS ta gida ce (FTP akan TLS). Ba ta ƙirƙirar asusun gajimare ba.
Mai haɓakawa ba ya gudanar da bayan-gida da ke karɓar fayilolinka, kalmomin sirri, ko bayanan amfani.

## Bayanan da mai haɓakawa ba ya tattarawa

Aikace-aikacen bai haɗa talla, nazari, masu rahoton rushewa, ko SDK na bin sawu ba. Mai haɓakawa ba ya tattara, siyar, ko raba bayanan sirri.

## Bayanan da aka adana a kwamfutar ka

Saitunan aikace-aikace (ciki har da sunayen masu amfani da kalmomin sirrin FTPS, tashar sabar, iyakokin haɗi, da hanyar takardar shaida da kalmar sirrinta na zaɓi) ana adana su a wannan kwamfutar kawai:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\FtpsServerApp\settings.json`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/FtpsServerApp/settings.json`

Idan aikace-aikacen ya ƙirƙiri takardar shaida da aka sanya hannu da kai, ana adana ta a nan:

- Windows: `%LocalAppData%\FtpsServerLibrary\Certificates`
- Linux: `~/.local/share/FtpsServerLibrary/Certificates`

Ana iya rubuta rajistan sabar a nan:

- Windows: `%AppData%\ftps-server\logs`
- Linux: `~/.config/ftps-server/logs`

Ba a loda waɗannan ƙimar zuwa mai haɓakawa ba. Cire aikace-aikacen ko waɗannan foldaji yana goge su. **Fayilolin** da aka raba suna nan a foldajin da ka zaɓa; aikace-aikacen ba ya kwafa su zuwa sabar mai haɓakawa.

Ana zaɓar foldaji da zaɓin folda na tsarin. Aikace-aikacen yana raba foldajin da ka ba da izini kawai.

Ba a yi amfani da sabar mai haɓakawa don adana bayananka ba.

## Amfani da hanyar sadarwa

### Duba sabuntawa

Aikace-aikacen na iya neman sabon sakin GitHub:

`https://api.github.com/repos/drweb86/dotnet-ftps-server/releases/latest`

GitHub (Microsoft) yana karɓar buƙatar HTTPS ta yau da kullum (adireshin IP, user-agent, lokaci). Mai haɓakawa ba ya karɓar wannan zirga-zirgar.

### Sabar FTPS

Yayin da sabar ke gudana, tana saurare a hanyar sadarwar gida don abokan cinikin FTPS da ka saita su iya karantawa ko rubuta foldajin da ka raba, da sunayen masu amfani da kalmomin sirri da ka saita. Wannan zirga-zirgar tana tsakanin na'urorinka (da kowa a hanyar sadarwa da ke da waɗannan takardun shaida). Mai haɓakawa ba ɓangare ba ne.

Kai ne da alhakin wanda zai iya isa tashar, wadanne foldaji kake raba, da ƙarfin waɗannan kalmomin sirri.
